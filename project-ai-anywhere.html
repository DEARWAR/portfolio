The problem

Why this needed to exist

People constantly copy a message into one app, paste it into an AI tool, copy the answer and paste it back. It breaks the flow of conversation and wastes time — especially for people who write or reply in a second language.

The solution

What I built

AI Anywhere keeps the AI inside the conversation. It offers quick commands — fix, translate, improve, ask, reply — and a voice route (speech → transcription → AI) so the result appears in the same place you were typing, with no app switching.

Translate a message without leaving the conversation.

Fix grammar, improve, shorten, change tone, ask and more — with a custom-command concept.

Voice input

Voice → transcription → AI pipeline for hands-free use.

What I learned

  • Staying inside the user's current app is the whole product.
